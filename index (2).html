About

Making things
with my hands
& with words.

I'm a Miami-based artist and writer working in painting, ceramics, and literature. My visual work spans oil on canvas and wheel-thrown ceramic forms across glaze series.

In writing I move between poetry, fiction, and literary essay. I'm an MFA candidate at Florida International University, where I've studied with Richard Blanco, Campbell McGrath, and Denise Duhamel.

All works are available for purchase. For commissions or inquiries, reach out below.
